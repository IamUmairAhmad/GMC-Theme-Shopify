# Cart drawer

Open **Customize > Cart drawer** and add blocks.

- **Progress bar**: free shipping or gift thresholds. Per-currency thresholds are supported in Theme settings > GMC: Cart drawer.
- **Upsell products**: choose up to three products, a control style (Add button, plus, toggle, checkbox), and optional "only when the cart has these products".
- **Gift wrap**: a tick box. It saves the cart attributes *Gift wrap* and *Gift message*, and can add a wrap product as a line item.
- **Cart note**, **text with icon**, **discount code** and more.
- Drawer width, image size, title size and compact variant text are in Theme settings > GMC: Cart drawer.

The cart page has the same discount field as a block of the Cart footer section.
